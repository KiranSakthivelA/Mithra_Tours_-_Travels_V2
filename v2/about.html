About Us

Travel Built on Trust. Journeys Built Around You.

Mithra Tours & Travels, established in 2024 and based in Kilpauk Garden, Chennai, is a travel and mobility solutions company with a strong focus on corporate vehicle rentals and ground transportation.

What began as a focused corporate cab service has grown into a broader travel partner, offering corporate mobility, airport transfers, outstation travel, business tours and travel & holiday services.

Our approach is simple — safe vehicles, verified drivers, professional operations and a consistent experience on every journey.
